06 / Good to know

Clear answers
before we begin.

What services do you offer?+

WhatsApp chatbot automation, business websites, logo and graphic design, edited product videos and photos, social media content, and Meta Ads campaign support.

What can a WhatsApp chatbot help with?+

It can answer approved questions, guide product discovery, share links, collect order or booking details, send opted-in updates, and route a conversation to your team. The exact flow depends on your business setup.

What can a social campaign include?+

Depending on the agreed scope, we can edit product or demonstration videos and photos, prepare social posts, and plan or run Meta Ads. You approve the content, target audience, and budget before a paid campaign begins.

Can you guarantee more sales or followers?+

No. We create and run the agreed work, but results depend on factors such as the offer, content, audience, budget, platform delivery, and customer choices.

Can I request just one service?+

Yes. Tell us what you need and we can discuss a project focused on one service or a combination, then agree the scope before work begins.
